# Governance

Work: PP-SPEC-091 Proof of Persistence
Maintainer: Craig Thomas Ellrod (Proof Protocol)

## Who governs

Proof Protocol is governed by the Proof Economy Standards Alliance (PESA), practitioner-led and vendor-neutral ([proofeconomy.foundation](https://proofeconomy.foundation)). Until the PESA structure is established, Craig Thomas Ellrod maintains this specification as part of Proof Protocol.

1. **Vendors may contribute. They do not govern.** A party with a commercial stake in Determinations under this specification may file issues and propose text. It may not set or vote on the requirements it is measured against.
2. **Editorial, not judicial.** The maintainer is responsible for the editorial text. Whether a declaration, Determination or record conforms is determined from its records against the normative text, and is not decided by the maintainer (SPEC Section 14).
3. **Specification and certification are separate.** This specification does not certify anyone. Certification, where it exists, is a separate determination under PP-SPEC-009.

## How the text changes

1. **Issues first.** Any proposed change starts as a public issue on this repository.
2. **Normative changes.** A change to MUST, SHOULD or MAY text in SPEC.md requires the maintainer's written agreement and a new version.
3. **Versions.** Versions follow `major.minor.patch`. A patch fixes wording without changing meaning. A minor version adds requirements or options. A major version changes or removes a requirement. Every version is tagged and recorded in CHANGELOG.md.
4. **No silent edits.** Released versions are never rewritten. History is the audit trail.
5. **Records outlive versions.** A Claim Record states the specification version it was determined under, and is judged against that version.

## Transparency

Every version carries AI_DISCLOSURE.md, FUNDING.md, REVIEWERS.md and ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md, kept current before any release is tagged.

## Ethics

Everyone who maintains, contributes to, funds, or relies on this specification follows the Proof Economy Code of Ethics (ETHICS.md). PESA may terminate a license grant for non-compliance under LICENSE.md.
