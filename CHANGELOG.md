# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-04)
- Initial draft. Proof of Persistence defined as the answer to one question: Does the efficacy still hold over time?
- Section 3: the question, and the rule that proof is a term of art.
- Section 4: Claim Declaration registered and anchored before any evidence; limits fixed at registration.
- Section 5: evidence basis drawn only from Conformant, registered Proof Protocol records.
- Section 6: mechanical Determination, `proven`, `refuted` or `insufficient`, with precedence.
- Sections 7 to 10: scope, validity and expiry; independence; records under the `POPS-1:record` tag; family Integration Points IP-C1 and IP-C2.
- Section 11: the Proof of X claim family, PP-SPEC-082, PP-SPEC-089 to PP-SPEC-099 and PP-SPEC-101.
- schemas/persistence-claim.schema.json.
